Hostel

Supervised, separate accommodation for students from far districts.

For students who cannot commute daily, the college arranges supervised hostel accommodation near the campus — separate facilities for boys and girls, each with a resident warden.

Mess arrangements provide simple, home-style Bengali meals. Parents receive the warden’s direct phone number, and visiting hours are kept generous.

Facilities

✓
Separate boys’ and girls’ accommodation with resident wardens.
✓
Home-style mess with monthly menu.
✓
Study hours observed every evening.
✓
Warden’s phone number shared with every parent.
✓
Hostel and mess charges are separate from college fees — ask the office for current rates.
