Payment & instalments

✓
Fees can be paid in three instalments per year — at admission, before Puja vacation, and before the annual examination.
✓
Payment accepted by cash, UPI, or bank transfer at the Accounts Office. A printed receipt is issued for every payment.
✓
Board examination fees are collected separately at actuals, as notified by WBSCT&VE&SD each year.
✓
Hostel and mess charges (for boarders) are separate — see the hostel page.
Scholarships can substantially reduce this cost. Many of our students receive Swami Vivekananda (SVMCM), Aikyashree or OASIS scholarships — see the scholarships page. The office helps every eligible student apply.
