Classrooms

Light, air and attention — the basics done properly.

Each year group has its own classroom, so routines never clash. Rooms are ventilated, well-lit and fitted with projection facilities for visual teaching — anatomy diagrams, dosage-form videos and dispensing demonstrations.

Class size is capped by our 60-seat intake, and attendance is taken every period. Parents are informed if attendance falls below the level required to sit the board examination.

Facilities

✓
Separate classrooms for Part I and Part II.
✓
