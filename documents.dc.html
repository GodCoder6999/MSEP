Documents Required

Bring these at the time of admission. Originals are verified and returned the same day.

Checklist

✓
Madhyamik (Class 10) admit card & mark sheet — proof of age.
✓
Higher Secondary (10+2) mark sheet and certificate.
✓
School leaving / transfer certificate.
✓
Aadhaar card of the student (and of a parent/guardian).
✓
Caste certificate (SC / ST / OBC-A / OBC-B), if applicable — needed for scholarships.
✓
Income certificate of parent/guardian, if applying for scholarships.
✓
Six recent passport-size colour photographs.
✓
Migration certificate, if the qualifying board is outside West Bengal.
Photocopies: bring two self-attested photocopies of each document along with the originals. The office can arrange photocopying on campus if needed.
