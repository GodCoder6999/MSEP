Scholarships

Government scholarships make D.Pharm affordable for most families. Our office helps every eligible student apply — free of charge.

Scholarships our students receive

Swami Vivekananda Merit-cum-Means (SVMCM)
Govt. of West Bengal. For students with 60%+ in 10+2 and family income within the prescribed limit. Covers a substantial part of tuition each year.
Aikyashree
West Bengal Minorities' Development & Finance Corporation. For students from minority communities meeting income criteria.
OASIS (SC / ST / OBC)
Post-matric scholarship from the Backward Classes Welfare Department for SC, ST and OBC students with valid caste and income certificates.
Kanyashree (K3)
For eligible female students continuing higher education under the Kanyashree Prakalpa.
College merit concession
A limited number of fee concessions awarded by the Governing Body to meritorious students from economically weaker families.

How we help

✓

✓
