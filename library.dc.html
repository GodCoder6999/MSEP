Library

2,000+ volumes, reference pharmacopoeias, and a reading room that stays open till evening.

The library holds textbooks for every D.Pharm subject — multiple copies of the prescribed titles, so students who cannot buy every book are never left behind. Reference works include the Indian Pharmacopoeia and standard pharmacology and pharmaceutics references.

A book-bank facility lends core textbooks to students from economically weaker families for the full academic year.
