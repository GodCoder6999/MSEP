Questions

Asked most often by parents

If your question is not here, call the admissions desk — someone will answer plainly.

All questions →
What is the last date to apply for 2026–27?
31 August 2026, or earlier if all 60 seats fill. Early applicants get preference for hostel accommodation.
Is there an entrance test?
No. Admission is based on 10+2 marks and document verification, as per council norms.
Can I apply before my 10+2 result is declared?
Yes — provisionally. Your admission is confirmed once you pass with the required subjects.
Is D.Pharm valid for a medicine-shop licence?
Yes. After D.Pharm and registration with the West Bengal Pharmacy Council, you are qualified to be the registered pharmacist for a medicine shop.
What is the medium of instruction?
English, with free explanation in Bengali. Bengali-medium students manage well with our study support.
