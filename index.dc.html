About the College

A premier institute dedicated to excellence in pharmacy education

Muktir Siksha College of Education & Pharmacy provides high-quality pharmacy education that combines strong theoretical knowledge with practical, hands-on training — nurturing skilled and responsible professionals for the healthcare and pharmaceutical industries.

Our vision is to be recognised as a leading institute in pharmacy education, fostering innovation, research and continuous learning.
