Eligibility

Simple, clearly stated criteria — check before you apply.

You are eligible if

✓
You have passed Higher Secondary (10+2) or equivalent from a recognised board.
✓
You studied Physics and Chemistry, with Biology or Mathematics, as subjects.
✓
You will be at least 17 years of age on or before 31 December of the admission year.
✓
Students appearing for the 10+2 examination this year may apply provisionally; admission is confirmed on passing.

Frequently asked

I passed 10+2 a few years ago. Can I still apply?
Yes. There is no upper age limit for D.Pharm admission. Several of our students join after a gap year or after other study.
I studied in Bengali medium. Will I manage?
Yes. Teaching is in English, but our faculty explain freely in Bengali, and study support is provided for Bengali-medium students.
My marks in 10+2 were average. Is there a minimum percentage?
You need pass marks in the qualifying subjects. Admission is on a first-come, merit-considered basis within the 60-seat intake.
Is there an entrance examination?
No separate entrance test. Admission is based on 10+2 marks and document verification, as per council norms.
