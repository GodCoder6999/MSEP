Sports

Two years of hard study need a release valve.

The college maintains a playground for cricket and football, and indoor facilities for carrom, chess and table tennis. An annual sports week each January brings both year groups together.

Sport at Muktir Siksha is deliberately informal — participation over competition — but the annual events are taken seriously enough to have a running trophy.
