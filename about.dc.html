About the College

Who we are, what we stand for, and why families across North 24 Parganas trust us with their children's education.

A premier institute in Gobardanga

Muktir Siksha College of Education & Pharmacy is a private institute at Jogen Mondal Colony, Kuchlia, Gobardanga, in the North 24 Parganas district of West Bengal. We are dedicated to excellence in pharmacy education — shaping skilled and ethical healthcare professionals through a single, carefully run programme: the Diploma in Pharmacy.

Because we run one programme, everything here — the laboratories, the library, the faculty's attention — is devoted to D.Pharm students. Batches are small, teachers know every student, and no one falls through the cracks.

Quality education

A curriculum aligned with industry standards and the Pharmacy Council of India’s Education Regulations 2020, taught rigorously.

Experienced faculty

M.Pharm-qualified teachers across six subject areas, dedicated to teaching rather than divided across many programmes.

Career-oriented training

Hospital training placements, professional guidance, and preparation for pharmacist registration with the WB Pharmacy Council.
