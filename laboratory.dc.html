Laboratory

The heart of a pharmacy college — ours are used every single week.

Practical work is half of the D.Pharm course, and it cannot be learned from a book. Our laboratories for pharmaceutics, pharmaceutical chemistry, pharmacology and analysis are equipped to Pharmacy Council of India norms and run on a fixed weekly practical routine.

Students work in pairs, with a lab technician and faculty member present throughout. Every student maintains a certified practical record book — required for the board examination.

Facilities

✓
Pharmaceutics lab: compounding benches, tablet machine, dispensing balances.
✓
Chemistry lab: fume hood, titration benches, analytical balances.
✓
Analysis: pH meters, colorimeter and instrumental methods setup.
✓
Safety first: eye-wash station, fire extinguishers, first-aid kits in every lab.
