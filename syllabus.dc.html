Syllabus

The D.Pharm curriculum as per the Pharmacy Council of India Education Regulations 2020.

Part I — First Year

ER20-11T/P · Pharmaceutics
Dosage forms and their classification; packaging materials; tablets, capsules, liquid orals and semi-solids; basics of good manufacturing practice. Practical: preparation and dispensing of common dosage forms.
ER20-12T/P · Pharmaceutical Chemistry
Inorganic and organic medicinal compounds — antacids, antimicrobials, analgesics; structure, storage and quality tests. Practical: assays and limit tests of official compounds.
ER20-13T/P · Pharmacognosy
Crude drugs of natural origin — sources, identification, active constituents and uses; herbal drug standardisation. Practical: macroscopic and microscopic study of important crude drugs.
ER20-14T/P · Human Anatomy & Physiology
Structure and function of body systems — skeletal, cardiovascular, respiratory, digestive, nervous, endocrine and reproductive. Practical: demonstrations, models and physiological experiments.
ER20-15T/P · Social Pharmacy
Role of the pharmacist in public health — national health programmes, immunisation, nutrition, first aid, health communication and pharmacoeconomics.

Part II — Second Year

ER20-21T/P · Pharmacology
Actions and uses of drugs on body systems; adverse reactions; introduction to chemotherapy and toxicology. Practical: demonstration experiments and prescription-based exercises.
ER20-22T/P · Community Pharmacy & Management
Community pharmacy practice — dispensing, patient counselling, OTC medication, inventory and shop management, digital health tools.
ER20-23T/P · Biochemistry & Clinical Pathology
Biomolecules, enzymes and metabolism; clinical pathology of blood and urine. Practical: qualitative analysis and routine clinical tests.
ER20-24T · Pharmacotherapeutics
Drug therapy of common diseases — rational use of medicines, therapeutic drug monitoring basics and patient-specific considerations.
ER20-25T · Hospital & Clinical Pharmacy
Hospital pharmacy organisation, drug distribution systems, sterile services, clinical pharmacy roles and medication safety.
ER20-26T · Pharmacy Law & Ethics
Pharmacy Act, Drugs & Cosmetics Act, NDPS Act, Drug Price Control Order and the code of pharmaceutical ethics.
Printed syllabus booklets are provided to every student at admission. The official curriculum document (ER-2020) is available at the library.
