Principal's Message

A word to students and parents from Dr. Amit Kumar Bhattacharya.

Dr. Amit Kumar Bhattacharya
M.Pharm, Ph.D. (Pharmaceutics)
Principal

Dear students and parents,

Choosing what to study after Higher Secondary is one of the most important decisions a family makes. I welcome you to consider pharmacy — a profession that is scientific, respected, and needed in every town and village of our state.

At Muktir Siksha College of Education & Pharmacy we do one thing, and we do it seriously: the Diploma in Pharmacy. Our classes run on schedule. Our laboratories are used every week, not kept for show. Our teachers know each student by name, and our office staff will patiently explain fees, scholarships and documents to any parent who visits.

Pharmacy rewards discipline. If your son or daughter is willing to work steadily for two years, they will leave us with a professional qualification, eligibility for registration as a pharmacist, and a genuine ability to serve their community.

My door is open. Come and visit the campus — see our laboratories, talk to our students, and decide with your own eyes.

With warm regards,
Dr. A. K. Bhattacharya
Principal
