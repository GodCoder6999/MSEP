Mission & Vision

What drives us every day, and where we are headed.

Our Mission
To provide high-quality pharmacy education that combines strong theoretical knowledge with practical, hands-on training — nurturing skilled and responsible professionals for the healthcare and pharmaceutical industries.
Our Vision
To be recognised as a leading institute in pharmacy education, fostering innovation, research and continuous learning.

The values behind them

✓
Integrity — honest teaching, honest assessment, honest fees. No hidden charges, no false promises.
✓
Ethical practice — pharmacists handle medicines that affect lives; we teach the responsibility before the technique.
✓
Service to community — our graduates serve the villages and towns of North 24 Parganas and beyond.
✓
Discipline — regular classes, strict attendance, and a ragging-free, safe campus.
✓
Continuous learning — faculty development, updated laboratory methods, and encouragement toward higher study.
