Infrastructure

Everything on campus exists for one purpose: teaching D.Pharm well. See it in person — parents are welcome any working day.

Classrooms →
Spacious, ventilated classrooms with projection facilities for both year groups.
Laboratories →
Dedicated labs for pharmaceutics, chemistry, pharmacology and analysis, equipped to PCI norms.
Library →
2,000+ books, reference pharmacopoeias, journals and a quiet reading room open till evening.
Computer Lab →
Internet-enabled systems for computer-assisted learning and scholarship applications.
Herbal Garden →
40+ labelled medicinal plants used in pharmacognosy teaching.
Drug Museum →
Crude-drug specimens, dosage-form exhibits and instrument displays.
Sports →
Playground, indoor games and an annual sports week.
Hostel →
Separate, supervised accommodation arrangements for boys and girls near campus.
Canteen →
Hygienic, affordable meals and snacks on campus.
