Frequently Asked Questions

The questions parents and students ask us most, answered plainly.

Admission

What is the last date to apply for 2026–27?
31 August 2026, or earlier if all 60 seats fill. Early applicants get preference for hostel accommodation.
Is there an entrance test?
No. Admission is based on 10+2 marks and document verification, as per council norms.
Can I apply before my 10+2 result is declared?
Yes — provisionally. Your admission is confirmed once you pass with the required subjects.
How do parents verify the college is genuine?
Check our approval on the Pharmacy Council of India website, and visit the campus — approval certificates are available for inspection at the office.

Course & examinations

Is D.Pharm valid for a medicine-shop licence?
Yes. After D.Pharm and registration with the West Bengal Pharmacy Council, you are qualified to be the registered pharmacist for a retail medicine shop.
What is the medium of instruction?
English, with free explanation in Bengali. Bengali-medium students manage well with our study support.
Who conducts the examinations?
Annual board examinations are conducted under WBSCT&VE&SD. Our college is an approved examination centre.
What is the 500-hour practical training?
After the Part-II examination, students complete about three months of supervised training in a hospital or community pharmacy. The college arranges placements.

Fees & practical matters

Can fees be paid in instalments?
Yes — three instalments per year. See the fee structure.
Are scholarships really available?
Yes. Many current students hold SVMCM, Aikyashree or OASIS scholarships. The office helps with every application, free of charge.
Is there a hostel?
Yes, supervised and separate for boys and girls, near campus. Ask the office for current hostel and mess charges.
How do I reach the campus?
About 10 minutes by auto or toto from Gobardanga railway station on the Sealdah–Bongaon line. See the contact page for directions.
