Computer Lab

Digital skills are part of modern pharmacy practice.

The computer laboratory gives students internet-enabled systems for computer-assisted learning modules, drug-information lookup and the digital record-keeping now expected in hospital and retail pharmacy.

The lab is also where our office staff help students file their scholarship applications on the government portals each year.
