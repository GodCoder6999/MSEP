Anti-Ragging Policy

Ragging is a criminal offence. This campus has zero tolerance — and a working system behind that promise.

In line with UGC/PCI regulations and the directives of the Hon’ble Supreme Court of India, ragging in any form — physical, verbal, mental, or through social media — is strictly prohibited inside and outside the campus. Every student and parent signs an anti-ragging undertaking at admission, and every complaint is acted on within 24 hours.

What counts as ragging

✓
Any act causing physical or psychological harm, fear or embarrassment to a student.
✓
Forcing a student to perform any act against their will.
✓
Verbal abuse, teasing about background, language or appearance.
✓
Online harassment of any student by any other student.
