Diploma in Pharmacy (D.Pharm)

A two-year, PCI-approved diploma covering drug formulation, dispensing and patient care — with emphasis on ethical practice, healthcare regulations and industry standards.

Course at a glance

ParticularDetails
ProgrammeDiploma in Pharmacy (D.Pharm), as per PCI Education Regulations 2020
Duration2 academic years (Part I & Part II) + 500 hours of practical training over ~3 months
Intake60 seats per academic year
Eligibility10+2 with Physics, Chemistry and Biology or Mathematics — full criteria
Fees₹ 65,000 per year — full fee structure
ExaminationAnnual board examinations conducted under WBSCT&VE&SD
OutcomeEligible for registration as a pharmacist with the West Bengal Pharmacy Council

What you will study

Part I — First Year
Pharmaceutics · Pharmaceutical Chemistry · Pharmacognosy · Human Anatomy & Physiology · Social Pharmacy
Part II — Second Year
Pharmacology · Community Pharmacy & Management · Biochemistry & Clinical Pathology · Pharmacotherapeutics · Hospital & Clinical Pharmacy · Pharmacy Law & Ethics
Detailed subject-wise coverage is on the syllabus page.
